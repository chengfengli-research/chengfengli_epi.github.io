# About Me

I am a PhD candidate at Edith Cowan University, Perth, Australia, specialising in nutritional epidemiology and chronic disease prevention.

My doctoral research investigates the associations between dietary vitamin K intake and respiratory health, including chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), asthma, lung function and lung cancer. I work with large-scale prospective cohort studies, including the UK Biobank and the Danish Diet, Cancer and Health cohort.

My broader research interests include cardiovascular epidemiology, healthy ageing and the application of advanced statistical methods to population health research.

## Research Interests

- Nutritional epidemiology and dietary assessment
- Respiratory health and lung cancer
- Cardiovascular disease and chronic disease prevention
- Prospective cohort studies and statistical modelling
- Healthy ageing and cognitive health
